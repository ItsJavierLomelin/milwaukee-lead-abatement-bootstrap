Bay View: planning lead-safe work on a worker's cottage

Bay View's small frame cottages and flats were built for people who worked near the lakeshore industry. After more than a century of repainting, the paint matters.

Why Bay View's housing raises lead questions

Bay View grew in the late 1800s around industry on the lakeshore, and many of its small frame cottages and duplexes date from the decades after. Houses of that age have been painted many times, and the exterior trim is where chipping usually starts.

Match the project to the paint

Porches, stairs and windows are where owners most often work. Before any scraping, test the paint and decide whether you want lead-safe renovation or abatement.

Planning ahead

Send the building's age, the surfaces involved and any earlier lead results. If the building has two units, say which one is affected.

Common questions

Are Bay View cottages likely to have lead paint?

Many were built long before 1978, so it is likely on some surfaces. Testing is how you find out which.

Do I need a certified contractor?

For paid work that disturbs paint in a pre-1978 home, yes. Ask for their Wisconsin lead certification.
