Historic Third Ward: planning lead work in reused buildings

The Third Ward's old warehouses have been turned into offices, shops and homes. Older buildings still deserve a lead check before work begins.

A warehouse district reborn

The Historic Third Ward is a downtown Milwaukee district of older brick buildings, many rebuilt after a major fire in 1892. Reuse projects have turned warehouses into offices, shops and apartments.

When the rules differ

Lead rules in Wisconsin cover pre-1978 houses and child care centers. A conversion to housing or a child-occupied use changes what applies, so describe the planned use when you contact us.

Plan paint testing early

Testing early shows where lead paint is before design or demolition. That helps avoid surprises once walls and windows are opened.

Common questions

Do commercial buildings need lead testing?

It depends on the use and on the work planned. Housing and child-occupied spaces fall under the Wisconsin rules.

When should we test?

Before design is finalized and before demolition or scraping.
