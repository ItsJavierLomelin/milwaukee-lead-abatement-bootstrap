Riverwest: lead planning for flats and duplexes

Riverwest is dense with older flats and duplexes. When two or more households share a building, lead planning is about units, stairs and shared exteriors.

A neighborhood of multi-unit buildings

Riverwest has many older duplexes and small multi-unit buildings close to the river. In such a building, the scope of any lead work should name the units, stairs and shared entry areas.

Shared stairs and porches

Porches and back stairs get heavy use and weather quickly. They are common sources of chipping exterior paint and dust that tracks inside.

Tenants and owners

If you are a tenant, describe what you see to your landlord in writing. If you own the building, start with testing and a risk assessment before deciding on repairs.

Common questions

Do all units need testing?

The scope should say which units and common areas are covered. A risk assessor can cover several units.

Who do I call about a tenant concern?

The Milwaukee Health Department has a program for lead concerns, and a certified risk assessor can investigate.
