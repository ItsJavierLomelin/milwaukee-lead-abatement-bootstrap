For service inquiries only. If someone is in immediate danger or you suspect acute poisoning, call 911 or Poison Help at 1-800-222-1222.

What to have ready

The approximate year the house was built, the rooms or components you are worried about, and any earlier lead reports or city letters. For a duplex or multi-unit building, say which unit.

If a child has had a blood lead test, you do not have to share the result with us. Your child's doctor and the Milwaukee Health Department are the right places for health questions.
