How to Hire a Certified Lead Professional in Wisconsin

Lead work in a pre-1978 home needs certified people and a certified company. A few questions make it easy to check before you sign anything.

What the state requires

The Wisconsin Department of Health Services requires individual and company certification for lead-based paint work in houses and child care centers built before 1978. That covers abatement, testing and risk assessment, renovations that disturb paint and clearance sampling.

Questions to ask a firm

Ask for the company's certification, the certification of the person who will visit, and which discipline it covers. Ask what the report or scope will include and who will take the clearance samples.

Check the Milwaukee layer

For abatement inside the city, the Health Department requires a lead hazard reduction project permit. Ask whether the firm holds one and who pulls it for your project.

Common questions

Can a general contractor do lead work?

Only if certified for the type of work involved. Certification is specific to the kind of lead activity.

Where can I look up certifications?

The Wisconsin DHS lead pages list certification information and training providers, and a firm should be able to show you its own certificates.
