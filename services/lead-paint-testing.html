Lead Paint Testing and Inspection for Milwaukee Homes

Is that peeling paint on a Milwaukee porch or window actually lead? Color and age cannot tell you. Testing can, surface by surface.

What testing can tell you

An inspector may use an XRF instrument or collect paint samples for a laboratory. Either way, the result is a list of components, such as window sashes, door casings, porch rails and trim, marked as lead-based paint or not. That list is what makes later decisions practical.

Milwaukee has a lot of duplexes, upper-lower flats and cottages built long before 1978, and they have usually been repainted many times. A fresh coat on top says nothing about what is underneath.

Inspection or risk assessment?

An inspection locates lead paint. A risk assessment looks at whether paint, dust or soil is a hazard right now. EPA explains why these are different services. If paint is chipping, window dust keeps returning or a child has had an elevated blood lead result, a risk assessment is usually the more useful first document.

Who can do the work in Wisconsin

The Wisconsin Department of Health Services says lead testing, inspection and risk assessment in homes built before 1978 must be done by certified people working for a certified company. Ask for the certification before you book a visit, and ask which components the report will cover.

Prepare for the visit

Gather the year the building was built, any earlier lead reports, and a list of the rooms where paint is failing or where you plan to work. For a two-unit building, say which unit and which shared areas, such as stairs and entries, are part of the request.

Common questions

Who can perform a lead paint inspection in Wisconsin?

Wisconsin requires individual and company certification for lead inspection and risk assessment work in houses built before 1978. You can ask any firm for its certification numbers.

Is a general home inspection the same as a lead inspection?

No. A general home inspection does not identify lead-based paint. A lead inspection is a separate service done by a certified inspector.

Do I need testing before a small remodel?

If the house was built before 1978 and the work disturbs painted surfaces, test or treat the paint as lead-based. Window and door work, scraping and sanding are the common triggers.
