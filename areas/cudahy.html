Lead Paint Abatement in Cudahy, WI

Cudahy began as a company town built around Patrick Cudahy's meatpacking plant in the late 1800s, and it became a city in 1906. Many of its houses date from the decades that followed, which puts much of the housing stock well before the lead paint ban.

Worker housing and old paint

Cudahy's early growth was tied to the plant and the families who worked there, and compact homes and duplexes from that era are still common. Original wood windows and porches have usually been repainted many times.

A lead inspection is the way to find out where paint is lead-based before you remodel or buy.

Rentals and turnover

With a lot of older rental housing, owners here often ask about documentation and cleanup before new tenants move in. A risk assessment gives a clear picture of dust and paint condition.

Describe the unit and what the problem is, and we will help you decide what to ask for.

Lead services for Cudahy homes

  • Lead Paint Testing and Inspection: Find out where lead paint is before a remodel, a home purchase or a change of tenants.
  • Lead Risk Assessment: Understand hazards from paint, dust and soil, not simply whether old paint exists.
  • Lead Dust and Soil Hazard Control: Investigate dust after renovation and bare soil near painted exteriors.
  • Lead Paint Removal and Abatement: Plan permanent hazard control with a written scope, containment and clearance.
  • Lead Paint Encapsulation and Enclosure: Explore specialized coatings or barriers when the surface is suitable.
  • Lead-Safe Window and Door Replacement: Address friction dust at old painted sashes, frames and doorways.
  • Lead Clearance Testing: Get the post-work documentation appropriate to the project and confirm the dust is gone.
  • Rental Lead Documentation and Planning: Organize a Milwaukee rental project and match the paperwork to what the city asks for.

Common questions

Was Cudahy's housing built before 1978?

Much of it was. The city grew from the 1890s on, so a large share of its older housing predates the ban.

What is a first step for a Cudahy rental?

A risk assessment before turnover shows paint, dust and soil conditions in the unit.
