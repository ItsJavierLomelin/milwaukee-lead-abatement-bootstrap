Lead Paint Abatement in Glendale, WI

Glendale incorporated as a city in 1950 and developed quickly after World War II, so it has more midcentury houses than prewar ones. Many were built before the 1978 lead paint ban, which means older windows, doors and trim can still hold lead paint.

Postwar housing and the 1978 line

The lead paint ban took effect in 1978, so houses built in the 1950s and 1960s can still contain it. They are less likely than prewar houses to have layers of it, but a testing visit settles the question.

Ask about the exact components, such as window frames and exterior trim, rather than the house as a whole.

When to test

Test before you scrape, sand or replace painted components, and before rental turnover. Testing shows exactly where lead is.

If you are unsure whether the age of your house puts you in range, start with the year it was built.

Common questions

My Glendale house was built in 1962. Could it have lead paint?

It could. The ban took effect in 1978, so a house built before then may have lead-based paint on some surfaces.

Is testing needed for a small repaint?

If the paint is intact and you are only painting over it, the risk is lower. If you will sand or scrape, test first.
