Lead Paint Abatement in St. Francis, WI

St. Francis takes its name from St. Francis de Sales Seminary, established there in 1845, and it became a city in 1951. A compact lakeside community south of Milwaukee, it has a mix of older houses and early postwar homes that can still have lead paint.

A small city with older homes

St. Francis is small and tightly built, with many homes from the early to mid twentieth century. Houses of that age often have lead in exterior trim, window frames and old interior woodwork.

Before a renovation or sale, check which surfaces are affected.

Planning small projects

A single window or porch repair can disturb paint just as a big remodel does. Ask for testing before you start, even on a small job.

Matching the problem to the right lead service saves a lot of back and forth later.

Lead services for St. Francis homes

  • Lead Paint Testing and Inspection: Find out where lead paint is before a remodel, a home purchase or a change of tenants.
  • Lead Risk Assessment: Understand hazards from paint, dust and soil, not simply whether old paint exists.
  • Lead Dust and Soil Hazard Control: Investigate dust after renovation and bare soil near painted exteriors.
  • Lead Paint Removal and Abatement: Plan permanent hazard control with a written scope, containment and clearance.
  • Lead Paint Encapsulation and Enclosure: Explore specialized coatings or barriers when the surface is suitable.
  • Lead-Safe Window and Door Replacement: Address friction dust at old painted sashes, frames and doorways.
  • Lead Clearance Testing: Get the post-work documentation appropriate to the project and confirm the dust is gone.
  • Rental Lead Documentation and Planning: Organize a Milwaukee rental project and match the paperwork to what the city asks for.

Common questions

Do small repairs need lead precautions?

In a pre-1978 home, yes. Disturbing paint on one window can make lead dust.

Where do I start in St. Francis?

With the year the home was built and a description of the surfaces involved.
