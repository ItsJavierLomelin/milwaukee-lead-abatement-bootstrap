Lead Paint Abatement in Oak Creek, WI

Oak Creek was a semi-rural town of under five thousand people in 1950 and incorporated as a city in 1955. It has older farmhouses and small early homes, with large amounts of postwar housing, much of which predates the 1978 lead paint ban.

From farm town to suburb

The city's growth is mostly postwar, with some older homes from the town's farm and early suburban days. The oldest houses are the likeliest to have lead layers on wood trim and windows.

Ask what year your house was built and which components are original.

Before you renovate

Disturbing paint in a pre-1978 home calls for lead-safe practices. Windows, siding and porches are the common triggers.

If you are unsure whether your house falls in range, start with the year built and a short description of the project.

Lead services for Oak Creek homes

  • Lead Paint Testing and Inspection: Find out where lead paint is before a remodel, a home purchase or a change of tenants.
  • Lead Risk Assessment: Understand hazards from paint, dust and soil, not simply whether old paint exists.
  • Lead Dust and Soil Hazard Control: Investigate dust after renovation and bare soil near painted exteriors.
  • Lead Paint Removal and Abatement: Plan permanent hazard control with a written scope, containment and clearance.
  • Lead Paint Encapsulation and Enclosure: Explore specialized coatings or barriers when the surface is suitable.
  • Lead-Safe Window and Door Replacement: Address friction dust at old painted sashes, frames and doorways.
  • Lead Clearance Testing: Get the post-work documentation appropriate to the project and confirm the dust is gone.
  • Rental Lead Documentation and Planning: Organize a Milwaukee rental project and match the paperwork to what the city asks for.

Common questions

Does a 1960s Oak Creek home need lead testing?

If you will disturb paint, test it or assume it is lead-based and use lead-safe practices.

Is a farmhouse more likely to have lead?

Older farmhouses often have more layers of old paint, so testing is especially useful.
